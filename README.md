# Vellum

A pen-first drawing and markup canvas for the iPhone Duo, with PDFs from Dropbox placed at their drawing scale, meetings recorded on the canvas, and jobs handed to agents on the laptop through Sotto.

The order it is built in:

1. **Canvas and sheets.** Ink, text boxes, measures, dimensions and frames on a real-world canvas; several Dropbox PDFs placed at their drawing scale; saved on the Duo and synced to Dropbox.
2. **Drawing index.** Search every drawing in Dropbox and open it in Vellum, with DWGs plotted to PDF on request.
3. **Jobs.** Send a canvas to Sotto's Watcher to summarize, create a drawing, redline a DWG, or follow a written instruction.
4. **Meetings.** Record a meeting on a canvas, or share in a phone call, and get a summary back from the laptop.

`CONTEXT.md` has the words, `docs/adr/` the decisions, and `AGENTS.md` how agents work here.
