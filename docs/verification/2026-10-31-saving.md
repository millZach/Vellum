# Saving verification

October 31, 2026, on `feat/crash-safe-saving` (#9, PR #22). The CI job **Duo simulator captures** took these in [run 41275421350](https://github.com/millZach/Vellum/actions/runs/41275421350) at commit bd0c41c. It ran on GitHub's hosted `xcode-27` image with Xcode 27.2 and the iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1), unfolded by `apps/ios/Scripts/capture-duo.sh`. Each capture is 2853 × 2007 pixels, which is 951 × 669 points at 3×. The ink is the Debug-only fixture (`apps/ios/Vellum/DebugFixture.swift`), a made-up pipe run with no real drawing in it.

## Kill mid-drawing and reopen (the test #9 names)

`kill_and_reopen` in the capture script does this:

1. Launches with `--ui-reset --ui-draw-fixture`. The app makes the canvas "Kill test (example)" and commits the fixture's 17 strokes one at a time through the canvas screen, 120 ms apart. That is the path a Pencil stroke takes when it lifts: `CanvasEditor.commit`, then `CanvasSaver`, then a write to the ink log. The app then leaves one red stroke open, drawn in the live layer as if the Pencil were still down, and never commits it.
2. Captures `saving-before-kill`.
3. Sends `kill -9` to the app's process. The simulator's apps are processes on the Mac, so this is the SIGKILL iOS uses, with no chance for the app to save anything.
4. Lists the canvas folder: `canvas.json` (169 B) and `ink.log` (1,947 B, all 17 strokes, about 115 B each). There is no snapshot, because the log is far below the compaction size.
5. Relaunches with no arguments. The app reopens the canvas that was open and captures `saving-after-reopen`.

`artifacts/2026-10-31-saving/unfolded-<capture>-<light|dark>.png`:

- `saving-before-kill`: the fixture's 17 strokes, and the open red stroke (a zigzag left of the tank).
- `saving-after-reopen`: the same 17 strokes in the same places, and no red zigzag. Everything but the open stroke came back. The status bar reads "Saved on this Duo".
- `canvases-list`: the Canvases tab, layout C, with four made-up canvases. Each row shows when it last changed and its stroke count. The selected canvas's cached thumbnail is on the right with Open, Rename and Delete….
- `canvases-none`: the list with no canvases.
- `canvas-empty`: a new canvas, now reading "Saved on this Duo" where it read "Not saved yet", with the back chevron to the list.

## Not checked here

- Power loss. A simulator cannot lose power. ADR-0014 explains the 3-second batched flush, and `InkStoreTests` covers a log cut at every byte.
- The Delete… confirmation and Rename alert, which need a tap the capture job cannot make. They are in the PR's hand-test checklist.
- Drawing with the Apple Pencil on the real Duo, and whether saving costs frames there. That is the TestFlight hand test after merge.
