## What CI proved

Run [42344908225](https://github.com/millZach/Vellum/actions/runs/42344908225) at b22f419, on GitHub's hosted `xcode-27` image with Xcode 27.2 and the iPhone Duo simulator (iOS 27.1), all five jobs green.

- **VellumCore tests (macOS)**: 579 tests, 0 failures. `SymbolTests` covers the following, each exactly:
  - a symbol's drawing as strokes at a centre and size;
  - the printed-size cases: a 3/8" bubble at 1/4" = 1'-0" is 18" across; in a 1" = 1'-0" scale area it is the same 18" on the canvas and measures 4 1/2"; on an NTS sheet it is 3/8"; off a sheet it is 18"; in a 1/8" frame it is 36";
  - placing as one undo step, with the tag joining it;
  - the place event and its undo step through the codec, a replay and a snapshot;
  - Save as symbol's normalisation, and placing back at the same centre and size gives the same ink;
  - the next tag number;
  - the `.vsym` file's round trip and its unchanged bytes;
  - Dropbox's content hash;
  - the resize catch just inside and just outside each of ½×, 1×, 2×, 3× and 4×, with 1.5× free;
  - the factor from the drag, and a caught 2× as one undo step.

  `SymbolSyncTests` covers the sync plan, and the engine against the made-up Dropbox: a Duo-saved symbol uploads; a laptop-added one downloads; the second sync is one listing and moves nothing; offline nothing moves or changes until the connection returns; a laptop change comes down; a change on both sides keeps both.
- **Placing on the 10,000-stroke canvas**: a gate valve adds 2 strokes (budget 2) in one change and one saved event. The renderer draws 204,678 pt² of tiles over the ink, 4 tiles, the same as a new stroke, and never redraws the view (budget 204,678). It took 1.7 ms in the test build, recorded and not compared.
