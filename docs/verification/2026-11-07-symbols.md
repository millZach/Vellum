## Hand test on the Duo, from the TestFlight build after merge

With the Apple Pencil, the Duo unfolded. Unchecked until done.

- [ ] Tap Symbols on the rail, then tap it again: the library flyout opens beside the rail with the six starters, each drawn with its name and printed size.
- [ ] Pick Gate valve and tap on a placed sheet at 1/4" = 1'-0": a 1/4" valve lands centred on the tap, upright, in the pen's colour; the status bar says `1/4" printed · 1'-0"`. Measure it: 1'-0".
- [ ] Place a gate valve inside a scale area (a detail at 1" = 1'-0"): the same size on the paper; Measure reads 3".
- [ ] Pick Instrument bubble and tap on the sheet: the bubble lands, the canvas brings it above the keyboard, and the pop-up beside it offers FT 101. Neither the pop-up nor the keyboard covers the bubble. Type 102 and Place.
- [ ] Place a second bubble: it offers FT 103.
- [ ] Pick the Text box tool, tap the tag and change it to FT 110.
- [ ] Undo: the edit goes. Undo again: the bubble and its tag go together. Redo puts both back.
- [ ] Pick the Lasso, loop a placed valve and move it; erase one of its lines with the eraser.
- [ ] Loop some ink, tap Save as symbol, name it, keep its printed size, Save. It is in the flyout; place it on another canvas.
- [ ] Lasso a placed bubble and drag a corner: the status bar reads `Scale 1.5×` and so on, and the drag catches at 2× with a light tap. Lift, then undo: it is back as it was.
- [ ] Airplane mode on: save another symbol and place it. Airplane mode off, leave and come back to the app: both new symbols are in `Dropbox/Vellum/Symbols` on the laptop as `.vsym` files.
- [ ] On the laptop, copy one `.vsym` under a new name in `Dropbox/Vellum/Symbols`; bring Vellum back to the foreground: it is in the flyout.
