# Verification: asking the laptop to plot a DWG with no PDF, and waiting for it on the Duo (#70)

Every drawing, path and reason here is made up: the laptop indexer's fixture index (`laptop/tests/fixtures/drawing-index`),
whose E-201 and M-102 are DWGs with no PDF, and a made-up Dropbox with a made-up laptop answering.

## What CI proved

- **VellumCore tests (macOS)**, `PlotRequestTests`:
  - The request's bytes are the example in `docs/plot-requests.md`, character for character and field for field, and a
    request for the fixture's E-201 and M-102 (and one with a `null` layout) passes a port of the laptop's `decode` and
    `request_name` rules: the id, the path, the layout, the time format and the file name. The port refuses what the doc
    says the laptop refuses, so its pass means something.
  - The stages: asked, queued offline, sent, plotting, waiting for the laptop at the check-in's limit (still seen at
    300 s) and just past it (301 s), failed with the laptop's reason and asked again as a new request, done with and
    without a warning, and done but offline.
  - One request per entry while it is open, through a relaunch too; two Duos asking for one drawing write two files;
    2,000 fresh names never repeat, and each is one the laptop takes.
  - Done: the PDF comes down once, through `SheetPDFs`, under the answer's content hash, and the request is deleted after
    it, never before (and not at all while the PDF's download fails). The answer is never deleted, and nothing is
    written or deleted outside `Vellum/Plots/requests`.
  - Waiting stops when nothing is open, and doesn't look while the app is off the screen or offline.
  - The counts in `docs/perf/baselines/plot-requests.json`: 1 upload of 165 bytes per ask, 13 requests a minute while a
    request is new and 3 after, 1 PDF download per plot.
- **iOS simulator build (macOS)**: the app builds unsigned for the simulator.
- **Duo captures: sheets and drawing viewer**, unfolded, light and dark (`unfolded-viewer-plot*-{light,dark}.png`):
  E-201 plotting on the laptop, waiting for the laptop, queued on this Duo, the laptop's reason with Ask again, the viewer
  filled in with its plot and the line about missing references, and the banner over the Canvases tab after leaving the
  viewer. Every earlier capture in the job is still taken.
