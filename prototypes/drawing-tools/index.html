Try these

  1. Pick Line. Draw one, then make it exactly 12'-6" at 30°.
  2. Start a line on the middle of the skid's top edge and end it on the side of the tank, where it just touches (Tangent).
  3. Pick Rectangle and make an 8' × 4' box. Then snap a line to where the two pipes cross (Intersection).
  4. Try Polyline (tap each bend, then Done), Arc (start, end, then the bulge) and Polygon (6 sides by default).
  5. On Draw it, sketch by hand as you do today: it still straightens when you lift.
