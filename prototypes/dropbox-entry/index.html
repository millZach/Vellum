Where Vellum connects to Dropbox

Two options, each shown before and after you sign in. Everything here is made up.

A — Drawings tab

The Drawings tab is where your Dropbox lives: connect once there, then it lists your folders (and later the drawing index). Add sheet on the canvas opens the same browser. Sign out is in Settings.

B — Only when you need it

No Dropbox screen up front. The first time you press Add sheet (or Send), Vellum asks you to connect, right there on the canvas. The status bar then shows Dropbox is connected; sign out is in Settings.
