Vellum: measure, dimension, frame

Issue #8. Four questions: pick A, B or C in each. In the drawings, one finger, the Pencil or the mouse uses the tool, and two fingers pan and zoom. Scroll from outside the drawings.

1Placing a dimension

Tap two ends to dimension them. Try a straight pipe run, then a diagonal from a skid corner to the tank centre.

2Finding the points

Zoom out with two fingers until the drawing is small, then measure a skid corner to the tank centre. The point shows while you are down and is taken when you lift.

3Placing a frame

Put a frame around the boiler house, then one around the whole site.

The site is about 240 × 150 ft. The plant room from the other sections sits top right.

4Rounding feet and inches

The header run is exactly 13'-7 5/16". See what each choice shows on it and in the table. Tap two points to dimension something else.
