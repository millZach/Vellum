The canvas screen you picked already shows the add-sheet flow. These are the four things it leaves open for building #11. Each section has a live canvas: pick A, B or C and try it. Your picks stay in the link.

Already settled, so not asked here
  • Add sheet sits at the right of the top bar, beside Record and Send, and opens the same Dropbox browser as the Drawings tab. Index search comes with milestone 2.
  • After you pick a page, a confirm step names what Vellum read: "The title block says SCALE: 1/4" = 1'-0". On the canvas it will cover 144'-0" × 96'-0"", with Cancel, Change scale and Place sheet.
  • AS NOTED or a scan: Vellum says it can't tell the scale and offers common scales to pick or type. Place sheet waits until you choose one.
  • NTS: the sheet goes on at its printed size, and lengths on it read as printed inches marked NTS.
  • Every sheet has a label with its number and scale. After placing, the view zooms to the new sheet and a short message confirms it.
  • Dark mode (ADR-0011): sheets invert, and a sheet that inverts badly shows dimmed instead. Sketch ink stays dark over light paper.
Pencil or mouse uses the selected tool. With Measure on, one finger measures too. Two fingers pan and pinch. With a mouse, right-drag pans and Ctrl + wheel zooms, or use + and −. Lengths are in feet and inches.
