Vellum: turn a placed sheet

Prototype for #44, nothing is saved. The sheet and every mark on it are made up. Pick in each question and try the working canvas below.

Try this

  1. With the lasso in hand, tap the sheet to select it. A sheet only turns or moves while it's selected, so a pinch can't turn one by accident. Today the lasso can't pick up a sheet at all.
  2. Turn it with whatever you picked in question 1. With a mouse, hold Shift and drag to twist.
  3. Watch the status bar: the printed 24'-0" must still read 24'-0" at every angle.
  4. Draw with the pen, then turn the sheet again, to see what your own ink does.
