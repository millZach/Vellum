Speed check for the Duo prototype, #42

The pinch over a placed sheet sometimes stalls on the simulator. Nobody can time it on the real Duo without a Mac, so this is a way for a TestFlight build to time a gesture itself. Three places the numbers could show; flip with the black bar at the bottom.

Site walk, Level 2
Pen. Draw on the canvas.

Pinch with the button, two fingers on the canvas, or Ctrl + scroll. The numbers are made up and play the same each time: 1.9 s at 120 Hz, 4 frames missed, worst gap 92.4 ms.
